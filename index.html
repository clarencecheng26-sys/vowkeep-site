The idea

Two lists. Nothing else to remember.

↑ What you need to doThe quote you said you would send. The call you promised on Friday.
↓ What you are waiting forThe requirements a client said they would share. The reply you are waiting for.

Amber (arrow up) is what you need to do, green (arrow down) is what you are waiting for. The words and arrows are there so it never depends on colour alone. Today shows both, with the overdue ones first.

The problem

You meet 30 people. You promise 10 things. By Friday, some of it has slipped.

Follow-ups end up in your head, in WhatsApp threads and in a spreadsheet nobody updates. VowKeep is for people who manage many relationships: sales, account management, buying, operations and advising. It is made for you, not for your manager's report.

A day with VowKeep

At an eventTurn on event mode and scan the cards as you collect them (Plus scans up to 10 at a time). Each card is straightened and filed under its person. Afterwards, ask for a draft follow-up for everyone you met (a Pro feature; Plus has one try a month).
After a callType or speak a note. VowKeep offers the promises it found for you to tick, and can set the next follow-up date.
In the morningToday lists who to contact, what you promised and what you are waiting for, overdue first. A reminder on your phone nudges you at the time you choose.
When a reply comes inPaste it in and ask VowKeep to read it (this uses AI). If it says the file is attached, VowKeep offers to tick the matching "waiting for" item off, and you confirm.

Questions

Common questions

The basics

What is VowKeep, in one line?

A mobile app that keeps your follow-ups and promises in one list, and reminds you at the right time.

Who is it for?

Anyone who manages many relationships: sales, account management, buying, operations and advising. If you often think "what did I promise them?", it is for you.

Is this a CRM?

No. A CRM is built so your manager can see your pipeline. VowKeep is for you, so you do not drop things.

Why not just use Reminders?

Reminders does not know who a promise is to, cannot scan a business card, and cannot tell what you need to do from what you are waiting for.

Can I bring my existing contacts?

Yes. Import an Excel, CSV or vCard file, or choose contacts from your phone yourself (it never reads your address book in the background). You can export your data to Excel at any time.

Which phones does it run on?

iPhone first, for Singapore and Malaysia. Android comes later: the sign-up form asks which phone you use so we know how many people want it.

Which languages does the app use?

English, and it is being translated into Chinese (simplified and traditional), Malay, Indonesian, Thai, Vietnamese, Japanese and Korean. You choose the app language in Me, then Languages.

Can it write messages in my contact's language?

Yes, when you choose it. By default the AI writes in the language of your notes. To write to someone in another language, pick that language once in the draft screen and VowKeep remembers it for that contact. It does not guess anyone's language.

Privacy and your data

Does it read my email or contacts?

No. It only sees what you choose to type, paste or photograph.

Where is my data kept, and who can see it?

In your account, on servers in Singapore, encrypted. Each account can read only its own data. We never sell or share your contacts. The Privacy Policy lists every provider we use.

What does the AI see?

Only what you choose to send, only when you ask, and only after you agree once in the app. Simple lines are understood on your phone and are never sent. The Privacy Policy lists exactly what each feature sends.

What if the AI gets something wrong?

You check names, dates and promises before anything is saved. Nothing is saved until you tap Save.

Will it send messages for me?

No. You read every draft and send it yourself, from WhatsApp, email or wherever you choose.

Can I delete my data?

Yes, inside the app. Export your data first: one Excel file with your contacts, notes, promises, to-dos and your card (card photos are not in it). Then delete single items, or delete your account and all its data.
