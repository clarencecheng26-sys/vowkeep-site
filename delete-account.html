Delete your account

Fastest: in the app, go to Me › Delete account. Your account is locked at once and permanently deleted after 7 days; you can cancel within those 7 days from the email we send.

Without the app: email vowkeep.app@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my VowKeep account". We confirm within 2 working days.

What is deleted

Your account, contacts, notes, promises, tasks, card photos and settings. Data is removed from our live systems within 30 days and from backups within 90 days. Payment records are kept as the law requires.

Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel it in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.
