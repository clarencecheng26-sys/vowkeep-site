VowKeep Privacy Policy

Effective date: [launch date]
Publisher: Cheng Ee Pei (Clarence Cheng), an individual developer in Singapore ("we", "us")

In short

  • Your contacts are yours. We never sell, rent or share them for advertising, and we never contact anyone on your behalf.
  • VowKeep never reads your email, messages, WhatsApp or phone address book. It only sees what you choose to type, paste, photograph, import or share into it, and nothing is saved until you confirm.
  • AI features run only when you start them, and only after you agree once in the app.
  • You can export everything, or delete your account and all its data, at any time inside the app.

1. What we collect

What Why Where it comes from
Account: email address or Apple / Google sign-in ID, plan status To sign you in and run your subscription You, Apple, Google
Your profile ("My card" and "What do you do?"): name, company, role, contact details, signature, a line about your work To show your QR card and to tailor AI drafts You
Contacts you add: names, company, role, phone numbers, email, office address, website, notes, follow-ups, promises, tasks, key dates, business card photos (the trimmed card and the original photo), extra details To provide the service you asked for You (typed, scanned, imported, pasted or shared)
Usage and diagnostics: app version, device type, crash reports, AI usage counts and the cost of each AI request To keep the app working, enforce plan limits and AI budgets, and prevent abuse The app

Feedback you send, with an optional email To improve the app and reply if you ask You
Which buttons you press and how often (never your contacts, notes or messages). You can turn this off in Me To learn which features help The app; you can switch it off

We do not collect your location, your phone's address book, your calendar or the content of your email or messaging apps. We do not use advertising or tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

2. Card photos

Card photos are trimmed and straightened on your phone: finding the card's edges, flattening it and evening out shadows happens on the device, without AI. We keep the trimmed card and the untouched original so you can switch back if the trim cut anything. Photos are stored privately in your account and shown only to you.

3. AI features

AI features only run when you start them, and nothing they produce is saved until you check it. Before the first AI use, the app shows what is sent and asks for your agreement; you can withdraw it in Me, and VowKeep keeps working without AI.

Feature What is sent to our AI provider
Card scan The text on the card, read on your phone. The photo itself is sent only if the phone can't read the card or you choose "Read with AI". Adding the back of a card sends that photo or its text, to fill in missing details
Read with AI, Capture, Log an update The message, note or screenshot you chose. From one contact's page, that contact's name, company and open items; from the + button, the names, companies and open items of up to 300 contacts, so the update can be filed under the right person
New task with AI Only the text or screenshot you chose
Drafts, draft to everyone, meeting prep, morning brief The details and notes of the contacts involved, their open promises and key dates, and your own "What do you do?" line. Office addresses are not sent. Your signature is added by the app, not the AI
Meeting minutes The transcript or spoken summary you chose and, when started from a contact, that contact's recent notes and open items. We keep only the minutes you save, not the transcript
Ask AI (search) A summary of up to 120 contacts (names, companies, follow-up dates, open items, key dates, latest note) and your open tasks, with your question
Voice input Your speech is turned into text by your phone's own speech recognition (Apple or Google); no audio reaches us

Short, simple entries (for example "call Priya Friday 3pm") are understood on your phone and are not sent to AI. The app shows "No AI needed" or "Uses AI" before you tap.

Our AI provider is Anthropic PBC in the United States. Under its commercial terms, data sent through its API is used only to produce the result for you and is not used to train its models [confirm against Anthropic's current commercial terms and data retention period before publishing]. AI results can be wrong: check names, numbers and dates before you rely on them or send them.

5. People in your contacts

You are responsible for having a proper basis to keep details about the people you add (for example, they gave you their business card), and for getting agreement before you record or transcribe a conversation with them where the law requires it. We process those details only to provide VowKeep to you. If someone asks us to remove their details from a user's account, we pass the request to that user.

6. How long we keep data

  • While your account is open, we keep what you store. Ticked promises and tasks stay visible for 2 months, then remain only as a line in that contact's history.
  • When you delete your account, it is locked at once and permanently deleted after 7 days, so an accidental deletion can be cancelled. Your data is then removed from our live systems within 30 days and from backups within 90 days.
  • AI usage counts are kept for 13 months; feedback and button counts for 2 years; payment records as long as the law requires.

7. Your choices and rights

In the app you can export everything (Excel/CSV), delete single items, delete all data, withdraw AI consent and delete your account (Me › Delete account). You can also ask for access to or correction of your personal data at vowkeep.app@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days. Deletion can also be requested on our web page: https://clarencecheng26-sys.github.io/vowkeep-site/delete-account.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, each account can read only its own data, card photos are private, the AI key is held only on our server, and the app accepts requests only from the genuine VowKeep app. If a breach is likely to cause significant harm, we will tell affected users and the Personal Data Protection Commission as the law requires.

9. Where our users are

We follow Singapore's PDPA. VowKeep is offered first in Singapore and Malaysia; where we add other countries we will follow their data laws too.

10. Children

VowKeep is for professional use by people aged 18 and over.

11. Changes

We will tell you in the app before material changes take effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.
