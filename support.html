Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?
On your iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › VowKeep › Cancel. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

How do I get my data out?
In the app: Me › Export to Excel.

How do I delete my account?
In the app: Me › Delete account. Everything is deleted after 7 days, so you can still cancel by mistake. No app? See Delete your account.

Does VowKeep read my email or WhatsApp?
No. It only sees what you type, paste, photograph or share into it, and saves nothing until you confirm.

Why did AI stop working?
You have reached a monthly or daily AI allowance. Your data and all other features keep working; AI resumes the next day or billing month. Me › Plans shows what you have used.

Restoring purchases on a new phone
Sign in with the same account, then Me › Plans › Restore purchases.
